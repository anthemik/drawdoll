Realistic body asset for DrawDoll.

Derived from the MakeHuman 1.1 base mesh, default skeleton weights, macro
shape targets and female breast-size targets (https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman), which are
released under CC0 1.0 (public domain). Regenerate with:

    python3 -I tools/build_realistic.py <makehuman-data-dir> public/realistic
